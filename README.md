# Cross-Update Stress Tests for Cosmological-Constant Mechanisms

## A Pre-Specified Framework Across Perturbative, EFT, and Physical-State Changes

This repository contains a machine-backed scientific framework for testing whether a proposed cosmological-constant mechanism carries the same claimed physical job across perturbative refinement, effective-field-theory or resolution changes, and genuine physical changes of state.

The framework separates three questions that should not be collapsed:

- protection or stability;
- empirical mechanism identification;
- numerical realization or value selection.

A mechanism may succeed at one without succeeding at the others.

## Why this matters

Claims to solve or address the cosmological-constant problem often combine distinct scientific burdens. This framework requires a mechanism to state what it actually claims to protect, what data remain free, what changes are being tested, what comparator is admissible, and what result would count before the target result is examined.

For physical-successor tests, the experimental burdens are claim- and route-dependent rather than a universal checklist. Required unresolved burdens remain OPEN rather than being silently treated as absent or failed.

## Scientific claim

The framework asks whether one registered physical job survives the changes a mechanism itself claims to control and, where the mechanism predicts a response to a genuine physical successor, whether that response is distinguishable from the strongest admitted comparator at the declared scope.

The worked example is original global vacuum-energy sequestering.

At the tested scope:

- matter-sector R₁ / R₂ protection survives;
- the tested QCD and electroweak local-history channels are not identifying against matched GR;
- the direct whole-history parent relation remains OPEN because the required complete spacetime history is not presently available;
- one registered linear-potential completion fails its stated joint cosmological role.

These are different scientific results and none is permitted to overwrite the others.

## What this is not

This release does not claim to solve the cosmological-constant problem.

It does not derive the observed value of the cosmological constant.

It does not identify dark energy with vacuum energy.

It does not convert compatibility into mechanism identification.

It does not treat failure of one completion as failure of an independently established parent protection result.

A clean Lean result does not establish the physical premises used to populate the contract.
