CCP Cross-Update Contract and Adapter Companion
Package Orientation
This companion is the human operating interface for the Cross-Update Stress Tests for Cosmological-Constant Mechanisms package.
Its purpose is practical. It explains how a specialist moves from a scientific mechanism and a frozen claim to a reproducible case record, how that record is represented in the CCP adapter, what the machine can and cannot certify, and what evidence would change the resulting scientific status.
The package has four distinct authority layers.
Physics and domain evidence supply the physical mechanism, equations, source definitions, approximations, transitions, calculations, observations, comparator support, uncertainties, and empirical evidence.
Cross-Update Stress Tests for Cosmological-Constant Mechanisms is the scientific anchor. It defines the scientific questions, cross-update discipline, comparison logic, claim limits, and worked sequestering analysis.
The CCP Stability Routing Adapter represents already-adjudicated CCP states, required burdens, support conditions, case licenses, and routing firewalls in Lean.
The Structural Flow Universal Kernel supplies reusable domain-neutral formal machinery used by the adapter.
The companion does not outrank any of those layers. It translates among them.
The governing direction is:
scientific registration and support
→ domain adjudication
→ machine claim license
→ downstream routing consistency
Never reverse that direction.
A machine-clean result does not make a physical premise true. Lean does not decide whether a source exists, a field-theory calculation is valid, a transition occurred, a comparator is scientifically adequate, a dataset is trustworthy, an exact mapping really holds, or a residual has actually been observed. Those remain physics-owned questions.
The machine checks what follows if the released scientific record has been faithfully populated.
The 22 Sₘ burdens are not a universal success checklist.
The Sₘ experiment ledger is a claim-activated routing surface. The first eight successor burdens are constitutive of every live Sₘ case; the remaining seats become required only when the registered route, claim, comparison, completion, or frozen conditional rule activates them. A burden genuinely outside the claim is not required. A required but unresolved burden is OPEN, not absent. Sections 3–5 carry the full requiredness and execution grammar.
The package is not an all-or-nothing test of “the cosmological-constant problem.”
The scientific anchor separates protection / stability, empirical mechanism identification, and numerical realization. Those questions can lawfully end in different states. Protection does not imply identification; failure of one completion does not erase independently established parent protection; and a measured value does not by itself establish a separate value-selection mechanism. Section 1 states the scientific distinctions in full. 
1. Scientific Orientation
1.1 The object being tested
The package begins from a specified mechanism M, not from the phrase “solution to the cosmological-constant problem.”
The mechanism must state the physical job it claims to perform.
For a joint protection claim, register a common protected functional:
Πₘ[X; θ₀]
Here:
* Πₘ is the quantity, constraint, filtering relation, global condition, or source-to-geometry response whose protection is being claimed;
* X is the class of physical inputs relevant to that claim;
* θ₀ is the declared reference theory data.
The same notation is not enough to unify unrelated claims. Every update included in one joint result must test the same physical job.
If one branch protects a radiative source relation while another branch merely fits an unrelated observable, those branches do not become one cross-update protection result by being written with the same symbol.
1.2 Three update classes
The scientific anchor uses three update tests.
R₁ — perturbative refinement
R₁ asks whether Πₘ survives a controlled increase in perturbative completeness for the same physical target.
The calculation order, background, renormalization prescription, relevant theory data, permitted running, uncertainty treatment, and protection criterion are fixed before the refined result is evaluated.
A successful R₁ result is narrow: the registered protection relation survived that perturbative challenge without an unregistered readjustment.
R₂ — resolution matching
R₂ asks whether the protected relation survives a controlled change of effective resolution, such as threshold crossing or EFT matching.
The higher-resolution description, matching prescription, inherited theory data, low-energy target, uncertainty treatment, and protected relation are frozen.
A successful R₂ result does not establish arbitrary ultraviolet protection. It establishes survival of the registered matching challenge at the declared scope.
Sₘ — physical successor
Sₘ asks a different kind of question.
A real physical state changes. The mechanism claims either that the same relevant physical account continues through that successor, that the protected baseline remains protected there, or that the successor produces a mechanism-specific response.
Sₘ therefore requires an actual physical successor rather than a redescription alone.
There is no universal Sₘ laboratory.
The laboratory follows from the mechanism.
A local pressure- or trace-sensitive mechanism may make a compact object relevant. An early-universe transition mechanism may require cosmological history. A global or historic mechanism may require global cosmology. A homogeneous-background mechanism may make a local compact object irrelevant.
The framework does not choose a convenient laboratory first and then ask the mechanism to fit it.
1.3 Claim profile is not the same as empirical adjudication type
The adapter records a ClaimProfile describing what cross-update structure the mechanism claims:
* fitOnly
* descriptionProtection
* successorContinuity
* successorProtection
* descriptionAndContinuity
* fullProtection
* unresolved
This answers:
What does the mechanism claim?
A separate human field must state the empirical adjudication type for the particular execution.
Examples include:
* deviation / mechanism-identification claim;
* empirical-protection claim;
* bound-only claim;
* one-way falsification / consistency claim;
* completion-viability claim;
* exact-non-identification audit.
This answers:
What is this execution trying to establish?
Do not collapse those questions.
A mechanism with fullProtection may be subjected to a particular experiment whose immediate purpose is only mechanism identification.
Conversely, an experiment showing one observable deviation does not automatically establish every protection claim carried by the mechanism.
The empirical adjudication type determines which downstream comparison, reach, and data burdens become live.
1.4 Protection is not identification
A mechanism can be non-vacuously protective and still fail to identify itself observationally.
If a mechanism fixes the relevant datum through its own equations or constraints, its protection result need not become fit-only merely because a reference theory can reproduce the realized observables after fitting an allowed constant.
That distinction is central to the package.
Protection asks whether the mechanism carries the registered physical relation through the declared challenge without unregistered retuning.
Identification asks whether the mechanism can be distinguished from the admitted comparator on the frozen channel set.
Those are separate results.
1.5 Exact non-identifiability is not inadequate sensitivity
The comparison sequence matters.
First ask whether the mechanism prediction can be exactly represented or absorbed by the admitted comparator over the frozen channels.
Then complete the broader comparator / degeneracy audit.
Only if a nonzero residual survives does sensitivity become the limiting question.
Therefore:
exact comparator degeneracy
→ NOT IDENTIFIABLE on the declared channels
→ sensitivity becomes non-required for that branch.
But:
nonzero residual survives
→ sensitivity becomes live.
If the residual is smaller than the frozen experimental reach, the correct result is BELOW SENSITIVITY.
These endpoints must not be exchanged.
Better precision cannot rescue a channel that is exactly degenerate.
Likewise, an inaccessible physical object or an OPEN forward model cannot be described as a sensitivity failure.
1.6 OPEN is not FAIL
A burden can remain scientifically live without being adjudicated strongly enough to pass or fail.
Examples include:
* an inaccessible required history;
* an unresolved source identity;
* an incomplete forward model;
* an unsettled comparator;
* insufficient data;
* an unfinished exact-absorption audit.
OPEN means the scientific burden remains unanswered.
It does not mean the mechanism failed.
This distinction is especially important for whole-history claims. If the relevant physical object requires information that is not presently accessible, the correct result is an open access burden—not a sensitivity limit and not an empirical failure.
1.7 Completion failure is not parent-mechanism failure
A completion is a separately identified scientific object.
If a particular completion fails its registered role, report the completion failure at that scope.
Do not silently promote:
completion failure
into:
parent protection failure.
A public negative result must identify both:
FAILED / BLOCKING OBJECT
the exact completion, branch, burden, or support condition that failed;
and:
PARENT / PARALLEL STATUS NOT CHANGED
the independently established result that the negative case does not erase.
1.8 Numerical realization becomes live only when the theory supplies the claim
A measured value does not by itself create a physical value-selection mechanism.
If a theory claims that the realized cosmological term is determined, restricted, statistically distributed, dynamically selected, globally constrained, or otherwise rendered non-free, register that mechanism and its decision rule as a distinct scientific claim.
If the theory makes no such claim, numerical realization is not a failed burden.
It is not live.
The framework therefore does not demand an answer to “why this number?” in the abstract.
It asks the narrower question:
What physical rule does this theory claim makes the realized number non-arbitrary, and what calculation or observation could show that claim to be wrong?
2. Registering a Case Before Lean
Do not begin by filling Lean constructors.
Begin with a scientific registration record.
The machine record is downstream of this registration.
2.1 Case identity
Record:
CASE IDENTIFIER
A stable identifier for the scientific case.
MECHANISM
Exact theory, model, construction, or completion being tested.
MECHANISM VERSION / SOURCE
The exact scientific source or frozen model definition.
CASE PHASE
One of:
* planned;
* in execution;
* completed / adjudicated.
The same case identifier should bind the scientific registration, human burden manifest, machine population, evidence record, and released execution.
2.2 Claim profile
State what the mechanism actually claims.
Does it claim:
* description protection under R₁ and/or R₂?
* continuity through a real physical successor?
* protection of the relevant successor baseline?
* some combination of those claims?
* none of them beyond a fit?
Map that declaration into the adapter ClaimProfile.
If the scientific source is not clear enough to determine the claim profile, use unresolved.
Do not choose a stronger profile because it creates a more interesting machine result.
2.3 Update class
State what kind of update is present in this case.
The adapter distinguishes:
description(perturbativeRefinement)
R₁ only.
description(resolutionMatching)
R₂ only.
physicalSuccessor
a real physical successor without a simultaneous description update.
mixed(d)
a declared description update and a real physical successor are both present.
unresolved
the update class is not scientifically fixed strongly enough.
mixed is a routing instruction. It does not create a fourth scientific update species.
2.4 Empirical adjudication type
State what this execution is trying to establish.
Examples:
* mechanism discrimination;
* empirical protection;
* exact non-identification;
* bound only;
* one-way falsification;
* consistency check;
* completion viability;
* another explicitly scoped result.
This field is human-facing but load-bearing.
It determines which downstream burdens become required.
Do not infer the empirical adjudication type merely from the ClaimProfile.
2.5 Common protected functional
Register:
Πₘ[X; θ₀]
State in ordinary physics language what it does.
Then perform the same-job test:
Would every branch included in the claimed joint result still be recognizably testing the same physical protection job if the notation were removed?
If not, split the case.
2.6 Free-datum ledger
List every datum on which the registered claim depends, including where applicable:
* integration constants;
* global variables;
* initial data;
* boundary data;
* fitted offsets;
* externally fixed constants;
* nuisance parameters;
* other freely selectable quantities.
For each datum, state whether it is:
* fixed by the mechanism;
* externally fixed;
* an initial or boundary input;
* inherited through a theory-owned map;
* or freely fitted.
A datum may not be silently re-selected after each update and still count as one non-vacuous cross-update protection result.
2.7 Source object and frame response
State what physical source object the mechanism actually answers.
Examples include:
* total stress-energy;
* trace;
* pressure;
* density;
* another operational total-state quantity;
* a specially defined subcomponent;
* a global average;
* a nonlocal quantity;
* a homogeneous-background object.
Where vacuum-offset sensitivity is part of the claim, state the mechanism’s response in the matter-coupled frame to the relevant source shift.
If a uniform source shift is not the relevant physical variation, say so.
Do not force every mechanism into one source decomposition.
A specially defined subcomponent must have a physical identity beyond bookkeeping at the claimed scope.
2.8 Actual Sₘ variation
For every live physical-successor case, register the actual physical variation:
ΔXₛ
State:
* the pre-state;
* successor state;
* physical transition;
* changed physical quantity;
* source object affected;
* and how the mechanism equations connect that variation to the registered protected relation.
The existence of a generic protection theorem elsewhere in the theory does not substitute for this connection.
2.9 Reference theory data and lawful parameter flow
Freeze the reference theory data:
θ₀
or a predeclared admissible region:
Θ₀
For each branch, state the theory-owned inheritance map:
θᵢ = Fᵢ(θ₀)
Lawful inheritance may include:
* RG running;
* EFT matching;
* state dressing;
* screening;
* global constraints;
* boundary evolution;
* another theory-owned transformation.
Distinguish lawful evolution of frozen theory data from branch-specific retuning.
2.10 Scale / state bridge
Register the mechanism-owned bridge Bₘ.
State:
* what physical content is carried;
* which equations or constraints carry it;
* which data are inherited;
* what degrees of freedom are integrated out or activated;
* what environmental dependence is allowed;
* and what uncertainty is introduced.
The bridge need not be a universal beta-function chain.
It must be the actual theory-owned route connecting the claimed physical job across the updates being joined.
2.11 Sₘ route
For a live physical-successor case, choose the route from the mechanism’s physics:
epochLocal
The relevant successor claim is tested through a local or epoch-restricted channel.
historicalProtectedTransition
A protected-sector physical transition or historical successor is itself the experiment object.
wholeHistory
The mechanism’s claim depends on a global or complete-history object.
Choose the route before target-result access.
Do not select a route because it produces the preferred outcome.
2.12 Channel set
Freeze the observable, dataset, calculation, or other adjudication channel set.
The channel set defines where the scientific comparison will be made.
It does not by itself establish:
* access to a required whole-history object;
* availability of a valid forward model;
* adequate data;
* or discriminating power.
Those remain separate burdens.
2.13 Comparator family
Freeze the strongest scientifically admissible reference comparator for the declared claim.
Include all allowed freedom relevant to the comparison, such as:
* fitted parameters;
* initial or boundary data;
* nuisance freedom;
* microphysical uncertainty;
* calibration freedom;
* other scientifically admissible adjustable structure.
Do not weaken the comparator to manufacture discrimination.
For an empirical-protection claim, separately register an unprotected comparator where the claim requires one.
2.14 Serious alternatives
For a positive mechanism-identification claim, freeze the family of serious signal-mimicking alternatives relevant to the declared identification scope.
The requirement is scoped.
It does not demand exclusion of every logically imaginable future theory.
It does require a scientifically defensible rule for why the alternatives included are adequate for the claim being made.
2.15 Exact-absorption and degeneracy rule
Before examining the target result, freeze how exact absorption will be tested.
The scientific sequence is:
1. test whether the mechanism prediction can be exactly represented within the admitted comparator freedom;
2. complete the broader comparator / degeneracy analysis over the frozen channels;
3. if a nonzero residual survives, proceed to sensitivity.
A successfully completed mappability audit is DISCHARGED whether its scientific outcome is:
* exact mapping established;
* or exact mapping excluded.
Likewise, a successfully completed degeneracy audit is DISCHARGED whether it finds:
* exact degeneracy;
* or a surviving residual.
The audit disposition records whether the procedure was lawfully completed.
The human ADJUDICATION OUTCOME records what that completed audit found.
Do not confuse those two jobs.
2.16 Sensitivity rule
Freeze the reach criterion before examining the target result.
Sensitivity is conditionally required when a nonzero residual survives the exact-absorption / comparator audit.
A canonical requiredness statement is:
CONDITIONAL (exact absorption is excluded AND a nonzero residual survives the frozen comparator audit).
If exact degeneracy is established, that trigger is false and sensitivity becomes non-required for the final branch.
If a residual survives, the trigger is true and sensitivity must be adjudicated.
The trigger rule itself may not be rewritten after seeing the result.
2.17 Result rule
Freeze what will count as the relevant terminal states before target-result access.
Depending on the claim, those states may include:
* PASS;
* FAIL;
* NOT IDENTIFIABLE;
* BELOW SENSITIVITY;
* OPEN / unresolved;
* a scoped bound;
* a one-way falsification;
* a completion-specific negative result.
Do not invent the result taxonomy after seeing what the calculation or data produced.
2.18 Requiredness freeze record
For each Sₘ burden, register:
REQUIREDNESS
as one of:
ALWAYS
CONDITIONAL(trigger)
NOT REQUIRED
For every CONDITIONAL seat, record:
* the exact trigger;
* the branch on which it becomes required;
* the lawful state if the trigger is not met;
* the pre-target version or freeze identity.
In a working machine snapshot:
* ALWAYS maps to some disposition;
* CONDITIONAL with unresolved trigger maps to some .open;
* CONDITIONAL with trigger true remains some disposition;
* CONDITIONAL with trigger false under the frozen rule maps to none;
* NOT REQUIRED maps to none.
Executing a predeclared conditional-requiredness rule is not a result-freeze violation.
Changing the trigger after target-result access is.
2.19 No-self-support rule
A terminal scientific result may not be used as the evidence that discharges its own prerequisite.
Forbidden examples include:
NOT IDENTIFIABLE
→ therefore mappability was discharged.
DISCRIMINATING
→ therefore the degeneracy audit was discharged.
PASS
→ therefore sensitivity was adequate.
the observed result was negative
→ therefore the case was a valid falsifier.
The lawful direction is:
frozen scientific question and method
→ independent calculation / observation / audit
→ adjudication outcome
→ terminal semantic classification.
2.20 Evidence record
For every burden used in a real case, keep a human evidence record containing at minimum:
BURDEN
Exact machine burden name.
REQUIREDNESS
ALWAYS / CONDITIONAL(trigger) / NOT REQUIRED.
STATUS
DISCHARGED / VIOLATED / OPEN / none as lawfully applicable.
SCIENTIFIC SOURCE
Paper, equation, calculation, simulation, dataset, preregistration, or other evidence.
SUPPORTED PROPOSITION
The exact scientific proposition that source supports.
EVIDENTIARY ROLE
One of:
* pre-result prerequisite;
* audit procedure;
* audit outcome;
* terminal result;
* post-result consistency check.
SCOPE
The physical and scientific scope of the proposition.
EVIDENCE PHASE
Where material:
* forecast / planned;
* achieved / observed;
* post-execution audit.
ADJUDICATION OUTCOME
For audit-style seats such as mappability and degeneracy testing, state what the valid completed procedure actually found.
One source may support multiple seats only when the supported proposition for each seat is stated separately.
Shared citation is not circularity.
Shared proposition masquerading as multiple independent supports can be.
3. Adapter Semantics and State Grammar
The adapter does not decide whether a cosmological-constant mechanism is physically correct.
Its job is narrower.
Given a scientifically populated case, it preserves:
what the mechanism claims
→ what kind of update is present
→ which burdens are live
→ what the domain says about those burdens
→ what result state is admissible
→ what positive or negative machine license follows at the encoded scope.
Physics owns the premises and adjudications.
Lean checks the conditional routing.
3.1 Top-level claim and update routing
The top-level routing layer uses three burden families:
descriptionProtection
successorLineage
successorBaselineProtection
These burdens are activated jointly by the registered ClaimProfile and UpdateClass.
Description protection
descriptionProtection is live only when:
* the claim profile includes description protection; and
* the update contains a description update.
A pure physical successor does not activate description protection merely because the mechanism also possesses a description-protection theorem elsewhere.
Successor lineage
successorLineage is live only when:
* the claim profile includes successor continuity; and
* the update contains a real physical successor.
This burden asks whether the later physical state really belongs to the successor lineage being claimed.
A pure R₁ or R₂ description update does not activate successor lineage.
Successor-baseline protection
successorBaselineProtection is live only when:
* the claim profile includes protection of the successor baseline; and
* the update contains a real physical successor.
A successor-baseline-protection claim cannot be live without successor lineage also being live.
That dependency matters.
Before asking whether a protected baseline survived into a successor, the case must first establish that the proposed later state is genuinely the successor whose lineage is being claimed.
3.2 Routing uncertainty
The adapter contains a routing firewall for unresolved claims and updates.
An unresolved ClaimProfile blocks cross-update routing.
An unresolved UpdateClass likewise blocks cross-update routing when the mechanism has registered a protection or continuity claim.
The exception is fitOnly.
If the mechanism registers no cross-update protection claim at all, then an unresolved update does not manufacture a live cross-update burden. The result remains NOT LIVE.
The machine therefore does not convert uncertainty into a stronger claim merely because a case record exists.
3.3 CasePacket
Top-level routing is represented by:
CasePacket
with three fields:
claim : ClaimProfile
update : UpdateClass
rawStatus : Burden → BurdenStatus
The scientific domain populates rawStatus.
Lean does not discover whether a burden passed, failed, is unresolved, or is not live.
The top-level raw status vocabulary is:
PASS
FAIL
NOT LIVE
UNRESOLVED
These are scientific routing states for top-level burdens.
They are not the same vocabulary used by the twenty-two Sₘ experiment seats.
3.4 Raw status and effective status
The adapter distinguishes the status supplied by the scientific record from the status that survives routing normalization.
effectiveStatus applies the claim/update logic.
The rules are:
Routing unresolved
→ effective status is UNRESOLVED.
Burden genuinely live + raw NOT LIVE
→ effective status becomes UNRESOLVED.
A live burden therefore cannot be hidden by simply entering NOT LIVE.
Burden genuinely not live
→ effective status is NOT LIVE.
Otherwise the supplied raw status survives.
This normalization is logical only.
It does not verify that the scientific adjudication used to populate the raw status was correct.
3.5 Aggregate disposition
The top-level case also has an aggregate disposition.
Its precedence is:
* FAIL
* UNRESOLVED
* PASS
* NOT LIVE
PASS requires at least one effective PASS burden.
A case in which every burden is NOT LIVE does not become PASS.
The aggregate result is deliberately coarse.
It must never be published without the component burden vector.
A public report should therefore state, at minimum:
descriptionProtection: status
successorLineage: status
successorBaselineProtection: status
aggregate disposition: status
An aggregate FAIL means that at least one live top-level burden failed.
It does not mean that every component claim failed.
This firewall is especially important when a completion-specific or successor-specific failure coexists with a surviving parent protection result.
3.6 Successor lineage and the PAA machine note
The adapter may reuse compatible Post-Authorization Answerability machinery from the Universal Kernel when representing successor lineage.
That is a formal lineage aid.
It is not a cosmological mechanism.
PAA does not:
* create a physical successor;
* supply transition dynamics;
* self-tune vacuum energy;
* determine a cosmological future;
* choose a history;
* or manufacture physical continuity.
Physics supplies the transition, the state identities, and the dynamics.
The machine checks only the encoded lineage relation after those premises are supplied.
3.7 SemanticPacket — the scientific result surface
A channel-specific scientific result is represented by:
SemanticPacket ChannelSet
It contains:
channelSet
admission
applicability
rawExecution
provenance
protection
channelRelation
evidenceDependence
These fields perform different jobs.
Do not use one as a substitute for another merely because two fields sometimes carry similar language.
3.8 AdmissionState
Admission asks whether the scientific case is sufficiently specified to enter the downstream adjudication at all.
The machine states are:
admitted
or:
notAdmitted(reason)
Current admission blockers are:
sourceOpen
shiftOpen
bridgeOpen
protectedFunctionalOpen
freeDatumLedgerOpen
Examples include:
* the relevant source object is not identified;
* the actual shift or physical variation is unresolved;
* the scale/state bridge is missing;
* Πₘ is not sufficiently specified;
* the free-datum ledger is incomplete.
NOT ADMITTED is not FAIL.
It means the scientific case has not yet earned entry into the downstream execution surface.
3.9 ApplicabilityState
Applicability asks whether the admitted mechanism and registered laboratory actually support the proposed test.
The machine states are:
live
notLive
outOfDomain
notIdentifiable
live
The registered mechanism makes a relevant claim in the proposed domain and the route remains scientifically live.
notLive
No relevant registered claim exists at that branch or scope.
outOfDomain
The proposed laboratory or regime lies outside the mechanism’s admitted domain.
This is not the same as a negative experiment.
If the theory does not apply there, failure of the proposed laboratory does not test the mechanism.
notIdentifiable
The mechanism is exactly non-identifiable against the admitted comparator over the frozen channel set under the registered comparison.
This is channel-relative.
It does not mean universal observational equivalence.
3.10 ExecutionState
The raw execution vocabulary is:
notRun
pass
fail
unresolved(reason)
Current execution blockers are:
belowSensitivity
forwardModelOpen
insufficientData
theoryDependenceUnmodeled
channelOpen
rawExecution is the state supplied by the domain adjudication.
effectiveExecution is the state after admission and applicability gates are applied.
Only:
ADMITTED + LIVE
allows the raw execution result to survive.
Every other admission/applicability combination normalizes effective execution to:
notRun
This creates several important firewalls.
NOT ADMITTED cannot become execution PASS.
OUT OF DOMAIN cannot become execution PASS.
NOT LIVE cannot become execution PASS.
NOT IDENTIFIABLE cannot become execution PASS.
A user can syntactically type a raw PASS into one of those cases, but the adapter does not permit that raw value to become an effective PASS.
3.11 Result / Evidence Provenance
The Lean type is named:
PassProvenance
The human job is broader, so this companion refers to it as:
RESULT / EVIDENCE PROVENANCE
Its fields are:
structural
computational
empirical
More than one may be true.
These fields identify the evidentiary route supporting the populated adjudication.
They do not mean that the overall result must be PASS.
For example, a controlled computation may support a scientifically meaningful FAIL.
Do not read PassProvenance as “provenance exists only for positive results.”
The name is retained in Lean for compatibility.
3.12 ProtectionNonVacuity
The protection field distinguishes:
nonvacuous
fitOnly
open
nonvacuous
The mechanism’s own equations, constraints, inheritance rules, or other registered structure fix the relevant protection strongly enough that the result is not produced merely by independently re-fitting the relevant datum after the update.
fitOnly
The result depends on free re-selection in a way that does not support the registered non-vacuous protection claim.
open
The distinction has not yet been adjudicated strongly enough.
A FIT-ONLY case cannot satisfy the adapter’s structural-protection PASS predicate.
3.13 ChannelRelation
The channel relation describes the relation between M and the admitted comparator on the frozen channel set.
The current states are:
discriminating
constraintFixedChannelEquivalent
belowSensitivity
forwardModelOpen
channelOpen
discriminating
M and the admitted comparator differ at the registered criterion over the frozen channels.
constraintFixedChannelEquivalent
M fixes or restricts the relevant datum through its own mechanism, but the resulting prediction is exactly reproduced on the frozen channels by the admitted reference description with allowed fitted freedom.
This is deliberately not fitOnly.
The mechanism may be structurally non-vacuous while still being observationally non-identifying on the declared channels.
belowSensitivity
A nonzero difference survives, but it lies below the registered reach.
forwardModelOpen
The relevant prediction cannot yet be generated strongly enough to complete the comparison.
channelOpen
A required observational, calculational, historical, or other adjudication channel remains unavailable or unclosed.
ExecutionState and ChannelRelation sometimes use similar words.
They are not the same field.
Execution asks what state the registered execution occupies.
Channel relation asks what relation currently holds between the mechanism and comparator on the frozen channel set.
Lean does not derive one automatically from the other.
The scientific record must populate them coherently.
3.14 EvidenceDependence
The evidence-dependence states are:
independent
partiallyShared
sameStructuralIdentity
This field prevents rhetorical double-counting.
R₁ and R₂ may each be legitimate distinct update tests while both results depend on one underlying exact identity.
That does not collapse the tests into one branch.
It does mean they should not be advertised as two independent mechanisms of confirmation.
EvidenceDependence is not a statistical covariance model.
It is an evidentiary lineage record.
3.15 StructuralProtectionPass
At the SemanticPacket level, a structural-protection PASS requires:
* effective execution = PASS;
* structural provenance = true;
* protection = nonvacuous.
It does not require empirical channel discrimination.
Therefore:
structural protection PASS
≠
empirical discrimination PASS
The adapter contains this firewall explicitly.
3.16 EmpiricalDiscriminationPass
A SemanticPacket satisfies empirical discrimination only when:
* effective execution = PASS;
* empirical provenance = true;
* channelRelation = discriminating.
This is a packet classifier.
It is not an independent proof that the underlying experiment really discriminated the mechanism.
The domain supplies the scientific adjudication.
The machine checks whether the supplied state satisfies the encoded classifier.
3.17 Two different status grammars
The adapter intentionally uses two status systems.
Top-level routing
PASS

NOT LIVE
UNRESOLVED

DISCHARGED

or:

3.18 The three Sₘ routes
The adapter contains three physical-successor routes.
epochLocal
A successor claim is evaluated through a declared local or epoch-restricted channel.
historicalProtectedTransition
A protected-sector transition or historical physical successor is itself the experiment object.
wholeHistory
The mechanism’s claim depends on a whole-history or global-history object.
The mechanism’s physics chooses the route.
The route is frozen before target-result inspection.
A route label is not evidence that the route is viable.
It only identifies which experimental architecture is being attempted.
3.19 SuccessorExperimentLedger
The Sₘ ledger contains:
route
and:

Each burden therefore contains either:

A seat is machine-closed when:
* it is none, meaning not required; or
* it is DISCHARGED.

This machine rule does not itself determine whether a seat was lawfully declared non-required.

4. Sₘ Experiment Contract — 22-Burden Dictionary
The twenty-two experiment burdens are grouped into four operational families:

9–12
III. Comparator, identifiability, and reach
13–19
IV. Data and execution integrity
20–22
The first eight are ALWAYS required for a live CCP Sₘ case.
Burdens 9–22 are activated by the registered route, empirical claim type, and predeclared conditional rules.
For every burden record:

* STATUS
* SCIENTIFIC SOURCE

4.1 realChange — Real Physical Successor
Question
Did a real physical state change occur, rather than merely a redescription, reparameterization, perturbative refinement, or change of effective resolution?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Why this seat exists
Sₘ is specifically the physical-successor branch.
A description update cannot become an Sₘ experiment merely because the calculation changes.
Admissible evidence
Accepted domain physics establishing the state change, transition, or actual physical variation.
DISCHARGED
A real physical successor of the registered type is established.
VIOLATED
The alleged successor is only descriptive or otherwise not the real physical transition being claimed.
OPEN
The physical status of the proposed change remains unresolved.
If VIOLATED
Do not report an Sₘ experimental failure merely because the branch was misclassified.
Route the scientific question through the appropriate description-update architecture if applicable.
4.2 protectedSectorMembership — Protected / Responding Source-Class Membership
Question
Does the changing source or state belong to the physical class that the mechanism actually answers?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Why this seat exists
Not every mechanism requires a special vacuum/matter decomposition.
The relevant class may be:
* total stress-energy;
* trace;
* pressure;
* density;
* another total-state object;
* a special source component;
* a global average;
* a nonlocal object;
* another mechanism-defined source class.
Admissible evidence
The action, equations, coupling rules, source definition, constraint, or equivalent theory-owned specification.
DISCHARGED
The physical object belongs to the mechanism’s registered protected or responding source class at the declared scope.
VIOLATED
The proposed successor object lies outside the class answered by the registered mechanism.
OPEN
Source membership or ownership remains unresolved.
Special-component firewall
If the mechanism acts on a specially separated subcomponent, its identity must be physically established beyond bookkeeping and fixed before target-result access.
4.3 piRelevantChange — Πₘ-Relevant Physical Change
Question
Does the successor actually alter an input relevant to Πₘ or to the physical state on which the registered protected relation depends?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Neighbor distinction
protectedSectorMembership asks:
Is this the kind of object M answers?
piRelevantChange asks:
Did this particular successor change something relevant to Πₘ?
An object can belong to the responding source class while a particular variation remains irrelevant to the protected job.
Admissible evidence
Πₘ, the governing equations, source-response relation, or another theory-owned connection.
DISCHARGED
The real successor alters a physical input relevant to the registered protected relation.
VIOLATED
The successor occurs, but the changed quantity is irrelevant to the registered Πₘ burden.
OPEN
Relevance has not been established.
If not discharged
The proposed successor does not test the registered protection claim.
4.4 mechanismApplicability — Mechanism Applicability
Question
Does M apply in the proposed environment, epoch, density regime, scale, state, or laboratory?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Neighbor distinction
This is a ledger burden.
It is not identical to the SemanticPacket ApplicabilityState.
The ledger seat records the evidence-bearing burden.
The SemanticPacket records the resulting scientific state.
Admissible evidence
The mechanism’s domain assumptions, controlled approximation regime, equations, validity conditions, and accepted domain evidence.
DISCHARGED
The proposed laboratory lies within the mechanism’s scientifically admitted domain.
VIOLATED
The proposed laboratory lies outside the mechanism’s applicable domain.
OPEN
Applicability remains unresolved.
If VIOLATED
The proposed laboratory cannot adjudicate that mechanism-specific claim.
Do not interpret an out-of-domain calculation as a negative test of the mechanism.
4.5 inheritedTheoryData — Inherited Theory Data
Question
Does the branch inherit the same frozen theory data through a lawful theory-owned map?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Job
Prevent separate branch-specific parameter choices from masquerading as one cross-update result.
Admissible evidence
The registered map θᵢ = Fᵢ(θ₀), including where applicable:
* RG running;
* EFT matching;
* screening;
* state dressing;
* global constraints;
* boundary evolution;
* another theory-owned inheritance rule.
DISCHARGED
The branch data derive from θ₀ or Θ₀ according to the predeclared theory-owned rule.
VIOLATED
A new independent branch-specific choice or retuning is required.
OPEN
The inheritance map is not controlled strongly enough.
Neighbor distinction
inheritedTheoryData asks whether the same theory ancestry is preserved.
freeDatumStability asks whether relevant free data are reselected after the update.
They are related but distinct.
4.6 variationConnection — Successor Variation Connection
Question
Do the mechanism equations actually connect the real successor variation ΔXₛ to the registered protected relation?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Why this seat exists
A mechanism may possess a valid protection result for one type of change while the proposed physical successor presents a different physical variation.
Uniform source-shift protection, for example, does not automatically establish response to a localized, time-dependent, gradient-sensitive, screened, or symmetry-changing successor.
Admissible evidence
The mechanism equations or constraints evaluated for the actual registered ΔXₛ.
DISCHARGED
The real physical variation is connected to the claimed protection or response.
VIOLATED
The mechanism does not answer that variation in the way the proposed Sₘ test requires.
OPEN
The connection remains unresolved.
If not discharged
An unrelated protection theorem cannot stand in for the physical-successor test.
4.7 falsificationConnection — Prospective Claim/Falsifier Connection
Question
Before target-result access, would failure of the registered Sₘ prediction actually defeat or narrow the registered CCP claim?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Why this seat exists
A mechanism may make interesting phenomenological predictions that are not actually load-bearing for its cosmological-constant protection claim.
A failed side prediction is not automatically a failed CCP mechanism.
Admissible evidence
The frozen claim profile, equations, physical role of the Sₘ response, and predeclared relationship between the proposed result and the claim.
DISCHARGED
Before seeing the result, the experiment is already a genuine test of the registered CCP claim.
VIOLATED
The proposed phenomenon could fail while the registered protection claim survives untouched.
OPEN
The relation between experimental failure and the CCP claim is unclear.
Anti-circularity rule
Do not discharge this seat because the observed result later happened to be negative.
The falsification connection must be prospective.
4.8 freeDatumStability — Free-Datum Stability
Question
Are the free data relevant to Πₘ held fixed, externally fixed, mechanism-fixed, or lawfully inherited under the frozen rules across the update?
Requiredness
ALWAYS.
Admissible evidence
The free-datum ledger and governing equations or constraints.
DISCHARGED
No post-update free re-selection is needed to obtain the claimed result.
VIOLATED
The apparent protection depends on refitting or reselecting a relevant free datum after the update.
OPEN
The free-data status remains unresolved.
If VIOLATED
A non-vacuous protection claim is blocked at that scope.
4.9 completionIdentity — Completion Identity
Question
If the case tests a completion, is the proposed completion scientifically identified as the parent mechanism or as the particular predeclared extension whose result is being attributed?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Usually NOT REQUIRED for a parent-only case that does not adjudicate a completion.
Required when a result is being attributed to a specific completion.
Admissible evidence
The completion action, equations, field content, parameter inheritance, frozen model definition, and relationship to the parent mechanism.
DISCHARGED
The completion under test is clearly identified and its inheritance relation is fixed.
VIOLATED
The tested construction is not the completion to which the claimed result is being attributed.
OPEN
Completion identity or inheritance remains unclear.
Result ceiling
A completion result may be attributed only to the completion actually registered.
A materially different completion is a new case.
4.10 completionViability — Completion Viability
Question
Can the registered completion perform its stated joint physical role under the governing theoretical and observational constraints?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Required for a completion-viability case.
Not automatically required for a parent mechanism test.
Admissible evidence
Controlled solutions, calculations, simulations, and admissible observations for the frozen completion.
DISCHARGED
The registered completion remains viable for its stated role.
This is a viability result, not positive identification of the parent mechanism.
VIOLATED
The registered completion cannot satisfy its stated joint role.
OPEN
Viability remains undecided.
Parent firewall
A completion-specific VIOLATED result does not automatically erase an independently established parent protection result.
Always name the failed completion and the parent status left unchanged.
4.11 historyClosure — History-Domain Closure
Question
If the registered claim depends on a historical or whole-history object, is the required physical history domain both correctly identified and accessible enough for the proposed adjudication?
Requiredness
Route- and claim-dependent.
Presumptively required for whole-history claims whose test actually depends on the history integral or equivalent global object.
Two distinct questions
Domain identity:
What history does the mechanism mathematically require?
Access closure:
Can the observer or calculation actually obtain that required history, or a mechanism-owned equivalent?
Do not collapse them.
Admissible evidence
The mechanism’s history dependence together with the actual observational or calculational access route.
DISCHARGED
The required historical domain is accessible, or a scientifically demonstrated mechanism-owned equivalent closes the same burden.
VIOLATED
The proposed history object is inconsistent with the mechanism’s own required domain.
OPEN
The correct history burden is real, but the needed information is not presently closed.
Important
A required complete future history that is presently inaccessible is OPEN.
It is not BELOW SENSITIVITY.
Smaller error bars on a local measurement do not close a missing whole-history object.
4.12 forwardModel — Mechanism-to-Observable Forward Model
Question
Can the mechanism generate a scientifically controlled prediction for the admitted channels at the required scope?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent but presumptively required for any case making a prediction-level empirical or computational comparison.
Admissible evidence
A controlled analytic calculation, numerical solver, validated simulation, or other scientifically accepted theory-to-observable map.
DISCHARGED
A usable and sufficiently controlled forward model exists for the registered inference.
VIOLATED
The proposed forward model is shown to be invalid for the registered mechanism or domain.
OPEN
The relevant prediction cannot yet be generated reliably enough.
Result ceiling
A forward-model failure ordinarily defeats the proposed route or inference.
It does not automatically show that the parent mechanism is physically false.
4.13 channelSet — Frozen Adjudication Channel Set
Question
Is the observable, dataset, calculation, or other adjudication channel set explicitly frozen, physically interpretable, and capable in principle of carrying the registered comparison?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Presumptively required for empirical and channel-based computational adjudication.
Neighbor distinctions
channelSet asks:
What channels define the comparison?
historyClosure asks:
Can the required historical object be accessed through them?
forwardModel asks:
Can M generate a prediction on them?
dataAdequacy asks:
Do we have enough realized evidence on them?
DISCHARGED
The channel manifest is frozen and scientifically appropriate in principle to the claim.
VIOLATED
The declared channel family cannot carry the proposed comparison even under its own intended interpretation.
OPEN
The channel surface is not fixed or available strongly enough.
If VIOLATED
The empirical route fails.
That does not automatically mean the parent mechanism fails.
4.14 fittedComparator — Reference / Fitted Comparator Family
Question
Has the strongest scientifically admissible reference family, including all allowed fitted, nuisance, microphysical, boundary, and calibration freedom relevant to the claim, been frozen?
Requiredness
Required for claims whose inference depends on comparison against a reference family.
Why the human label matters
fittedComparator does not mean “one fitted scalar.”
The comparator can be a full model family with substantial scientifically justified freedom.
DISCHARGED
The comparator family and its allowed fitting/profiling freedom are frozen and scientifically adequate for the declared scope.
VIOLATED
The comparator is artificially weak, physically inadmissible, or omits allowed freedom material to the conclusion.
OPEN
Comparator adequacy remains unsettled.
Scoped-strength firewall
“Strongest admissible comparator” means the strongest scientifically admissible comparator under the registered scientific admission rule.
It does not require exclusion of every logically imaginable future theory.
4.15 matchedUnprotectedComparator — Matched Unprotected Comparator
Question
For an empirical-protection claim, is there a concrete unprotected comparator that predicts a distinguishable response under the same physical update?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Central for empirical-protection claims.
Not automatically required for a pure deviation / mechanism-identification claim.
Why this seat exists
A null result supports empirical protection only if an unprotected alternative would have produced a visible response.
DISCHARGED
A scientifically justified unprotected comparator is frozen under the same relevant update and predicts the response whose absence would bear on the protection claim.
VIOLATED
The proposed unprotected comparator is scientifically invalid or does not actually predict the required distinguishable response.
OPEN
The comparator or its prediction remains insufficiently specified.
Null-result firewall
* “Nothing happened”
plus
* “the unprotected response could not have been seen”
does not establish empirical protection.
4.16 alternativeCoverage — Serious Alternative Coverage
Question
Has the predeclared family of serious signal-mimicking alternatives been covered adequately for the identification scope being claimed?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Required for broad positive mechanism-identification claims.
May be non-required for a narrow one-way falsifier, bound-only claim, or comparator-relative exact-non-identification statement.
DISCHARGED
The registered alternative family is adequate under a scientifically justified coverage rule for the stated scope.
VIOLATED
A known serious mimic relevant to the declared scope was omitted or mishandled.
OPEN
Coverage adequacy remains unresolved.
Coverage ceiling
DISCHARGED does not mean every possible future alternative has been excluded.
It means the predeclared scientifically justified alternative family is adequate for the exact identification claim being made.
4.17 mappability — Exact Absorption / Mappability Audit
Question
Can M’s prediction be represented exactly inside the admitted comparator family over the frozen channels?
Requiredness
Required when exact absorption matters to the identification or non-identification claim.
Seat species
This is an AUDIT / PROCEDURE seat.
Its disposition records whether the exact-mapping question was validly adjudicated.
It does not encode which answer the audit returned.
DISCHARGED
The exact-mappability question was validly and adequately completed.
Possible ADJUDICATION OUTCOMES include:
* exact mapping established;
* exact mapping excluded.
Both are DISCHARGED audits.
VIOLATED
The registered mappability procedure itself is invalid, non-diagnostic, or incapable of answering the frozen question.
OPEN
The exact-mapping question remains incomplete or unresolved.
Critical firewall
Do not encode:
“mapping failed”
as:
mappability = VIOLATED
A valid proof that exact mapping does not exist is a successfully DISCHARGED mappability audit with outcome:
exact mapping excluded.
4.18 degeneracyTest — Full Comparator / Degeneracy Test
Question
After the complete admitted comparator freedom is profiled over the frozen channel set, what relation remains between M and the comparator?
Requiredness
Required for the relevant identification and exact-non-identification routes.
Seat species
This is also an AUDIT / PROCEDURE seat.
DISCHARGED
The full comparator / degeneracy question has been validly adjudicated.
Possible ADJUDICATION OUTCOMES include:
* exact degeneracy;
* non-exact but substantial degeneracy;
* a surviving nonzero residual.
All are compatible with a DISCHARGED procedure seat.
VIOLATED
The profiling, fitting, statistic, or registered comparison procedure is itself invalid or non-diagnostic.
OPEN
The full comparison has not been completed strongly enough.
Neighbor distinction
mappability asks whether an exact analytic or constructive representation exists.
degeneracyTest asks the broader full-channel comparison under all admitted comparator freedom.
One calculation may sometimes supply evidence for both.
The record must state separately which proposition it supports for each seat.
4.19 sensitivity — Reach
Question
After exact degeneracy has been excluded and a nonzero residual survives, can the registered observation or calculation resolve that residual at the frozen criterion?
Requiredness
Usually:
CONDITIONAL (exact absorption excluded AND nonzero comparator residual survives)
Prerequisite order
Sensitivity is downstream of identifiability.
Do not use it to rescue:
* exact non-identifiability;
* an incomplete mappability audit;
* an incomplete degeneracy analysis;
* an inaccessible channel;
* an OPEN history object.
DISCHARGED
The forecast or achieved reach is sufficient for the registered surviving residual.
VIOLATED
The nonzero residual lies below the registered reach.
This is the scientific basis for a BELOW SENSITIVITY result when all other required burdens are closed.
OPEN
Reach remains unresolved.
Result ceiling
Sensitivity VIOLATED does not mean:
* NOT IDENTIFIABLE;
* mechanism FAIL;
* or comparator equivalence.
Better precision can reopen a below-sensitivity case.
Better precision alone cannot reopen exact degeneracy on the same channels.
4.20 dataAdequacy — Evidence Adequacy
Question
Does the actual evidence package have enough quantity, quality, coverage, and information content to support the frozen inference?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Presumptively required for an empirical execution.
May be non-required for a purely structural or analytic branch with no data-dependent terminal inference.
Neighbor distinction
sensitivity asks whether the experiment can resolve the physical residual.
dataAdequacy asks whether the realized evidence package is sufficient.
A design can have enough nominal sensitivity but still produce inadequate data.
A large dataset can be adequate while the physical residual remains below sensitivity.
DISCHARGED
The achieved evidence meets the frozen adequacy criteria.
VIOLATED
The actual evidence demonstrably fails the registered adequacy standard.
OPEN
Adequacy has not yet been established.
Possible semantic consequence
Where the case is otherwise admitted and live, a data-adequacy failure may support:
unresolved(insufficientData)
It does not imply mechanism FAIL.
Forecast firewall
A planned dataset that would have been adequate does not make the achieved dataset adequate if the actual execution failed the frozen criteria.
4.21 dataConsistency — Evidence Consistency
Question
Can the evidence components, calibrations, reconstructed quantities, and theory assumptions used together in the inference be combined consistently under the frozen analysis?
Requiredness
Claim-dependent.
Required where a material multi-source, multi-calibration, or cross-assumption consistency burden exists.
Neighbor distinction
dataAdequacy asks:
Do we have enough usable evidence?
dataConsistency asks:
Can the evidence we have be lawfully combined?
DISCHARGED
The evidence components used in the final inference are mutually compatible under the registered assumptions and combination rule.
VIOLATED
A material inconsistency defeats the registered combined inference.
OPEN
Consistency remains unresolved.
Result ceiling
Data inconsistency ordinarily defeats the combined analysis.
It does not automatically establish:
* mechanism failure;
* comparator success;
* or physical inconsistency in nature.
The lawful response may be to split the analysis, repair calibration, narrow the claim, or run a newly frozen execution.
4.22 resultFreeze — Execution Integrity / Freeze
Question
Was the scientific question and its decision architecture frozen strongly enough before target-result access, and did the released execution remain inside that frozen rule or a predeclared lawful branch?
Requiredness
Presumptively required for confirmatory empirical or computational adjudication.
Pre-execution meaning
Before target-result access, the versioned freeze should contain, as applicable:
* mechanism identity;
* claim profile;
* empirical adjudication type;
* Πₘ;
* source object;
* theory data;
* free-data rules;
* successor definition;
* ΔXₛ;
* scale/state bridge;
* forward-model class;
* comparator family;
* alternative family;
* channel set;
* exact-absorption rule;
* sensitivity criterion;
* data selection and quality rules;
* uncertainty treatment;
* result rule;
* conditional-requiredness triggers;
* permitted adaptive or branching rules.
Pre-execution DISCHARGED
The required preregistration / freeze record exists and is sufficiently complete to constrain execution.
Post-execution re-audit
The seat remains DISCHARGED only if the released execution followed:
* the frozen rule; or
* a predeclared lawful adaptive branch.
OPEN
The freeze record is incomplete, unavailable, or cannot be verified strongly enough.
VIOLATED
A material unregistered change after target-result access altered the claim, evidence selection, comparator, channel set, analysis rule, decision threshold, conditional trigger, or interpretation.
Not every version change is a violation
Lawful changes include:
* predeclared adaptive choices;
* branch choices already encoded in the freeze;
* changes made before target-result access;
* transparent non-substantive correction;
* or creation of a new separately frozen execution.
What is prohibited is presenting a target-result-driven substantive change as though it belonged to the original confirmatory execution.
Conditional-requiredness firewall
Executing a conditional trigger that was frozen in advance is not a resultFreeze violation.
Changing that trigger after seeing the target result is.
The twenty-two seats now define the full human-facing Sₘ burden surface.
They do not predetermine the result.
They determine which scientific questions must be answered before a particular result is licensed, which questions may remain legitimately outside the claim, and where a proposed inference must stop when a burden is OPEN, VIOLATED, non-identifying, or below reach.
5. Running a Case
The scientific registration comes first.
The adapter is populated only after the mechanism, claim, update, requiredness rules, comparison architecture, and result rule are sufficiently fixed.
A useful execution sequence is:
register
→ route
→ admit
→ establish applicability
→ declare Sₘ requiredness
→ close support burdens
→ adjudicate comparator structure
→ execute where lawful
→ populate terminal semantics
→ run machine licenses
→ report the narrowest result
Do not skip forward merely because the expected result appears obvious.
5.1 Step 0 — Identify the execution and phase
Assign the case and execution identifiers before machine population.
Record:
* CASE IDENTIFIER
* EXECUTION IDENTIFIER
* MECHANISM / MODEL VERSION
* SCIENTIFIC REGISTRATION VERSION
* REQUIREDNESS FREEZE VERSION
* CASE PHASE
Case phase is one of:
* planned;
* in execution;
* completed / adjudicated.
A later machine snapshot does not erase the earlier scientific record.
Where a conditional-requiredness trigger resolves during execution, preserve the earlier snapshot and the frozen trigger that authorized the later branch.
5.2 Step 1 — Freeze the scientific case
Complete the scientific registration in Section 2 before target-result access and bind it to the execution identity.
Do not restate the registration differently at machine-population time. The mechanism, claim profile, adjudication type, update class, Πₘ, theory data, free-data rules, source object, successor variation where applicable, inheritance maps, bridge, route, channels, comparator architecture, decision rules, and conditional-requiredness triggers remain those of the frozen record.
If a required scientific object is not yet known, record it honestly as unresolved. Do not invent it merely to make the case machine-populable.
5.3 Step 2 — Populate top-level routing
Build the CasePacket.
Populate:
claim
with the registered ClaimProfile.
Populate:
update
with the registered UpdateClass.
Then populate the raw statuses for:
descriptionProtection
successorLineage
successorBaselineProtection
as scientifically appropriate.

8. How to Challenge, Refute, Extend, or Reopen the Package
This package is intended to be defeasible.
A skeptical specialist should be able to determine not only how to run a case, but also what evidence would:
* overturn a result;
* narrow a claim;
* extend the tested scope;
* close an OPEN burden;
* falsify a scoped branch;
* activate a previously non-live claim;
* expose an adapter defect;
* or place genuine pressure on the Universal Kernel.
The challenge must identify what is changing.
A new result is not automatically a refutation.
A new case is not automatically a repair.
A representation defect is not automatically a physics defect.
A physics disagreement is not automatically kernel pressure.
The package therefore distinguishes eight change types.
8.1 Public challenge / status-change manifest
Every substantive challenge or update should record:
TARGET
The exact scientific result, burden, case, completion, comparator, machine predicate, adapter definition, or kernel dependency being challenged.
CHANGE TYPE
One of the eight classes below.
HELD FIXED
The parts of the original case that remain unchanged.
CHANGED
The exact premise, object, evidence, route, comparator, channel, calculation, or formal representation being altered.
EVIDENCE
The new calculation, observation, derivation, proof, source, or reproducible demonstration supporting the challenge.
RESULTING ACTION
What should happen if the challenge is accepted:
* overturn;
* narrow;
* create new case;
* close OPEN;
* falsify branch;
* activate claim;
* repair adapter;
* or reopen kernel adjudication.
This manifest prevents a later result from silently rewriting the identity of an earlier execution.
8.2 Change Type 1 — OVERTURN / REFUTE
Use OVERTURN / REFUTE when new evidence shows that an earlier result was wrong under its own original referent, scope, and frozen rules.
The challenge must preserve enough of the original case that the target remains the same scientific object.
Examples include:
* a calculation used to support R₁ is shown to be invalid;
* the claimed EFT matching result was mathematically or physically wrong;
* a purported exact comparator mapping is disproved under the same frozen comparator freedom;
* a supposedly excluded mapping is explicitly constructed;
* the original data analysis is shown to violate its own frozen decision rule;
* a completion previously reported viable is shown not to satisfy its registered role;
* a completion previously reported failed is shown to have been analyzed incorrectly under the same registered model.
A true overturn changes the status of the earlier result.
It does not merely introduce a different mechanism, different completion, or different observational channel.
Overturning a protection result
To overturn an R₁ or R₂ protection result, show that the registered protected relation does not survive the original challenge under the original allowed theory-data rules.
A stronger statement is not required.
The challenger need not solve the cosmological-constant problem.
They need only defeat the actual protection claim being reported.
Overturning exact non-identification
To overturn a scoped exact-non-identification result, show that under the same:
* mechanism;
* comparator family;
* allowed comparator freedom;
* channel set;
* physical scope;
the claimed exact equivalence is false.
A valid surviving residual is sufficient to overturn exact degeneracy at that scope.
Whether the residual is experimentally reachable is a separate downstream question.
Overturning a completion failure
A completion-specific FAIL may be overturned if the negative result itself is shown incorrect for:
* the same completion;
* the same physical role;
* the same governing assumptions;
* and the same scientific referent.
A materially different completion does not overturn the old one.
It creates a new case.
8.3 Change Type 2 — NARROW
Use NARROW when the original result survives, but its justified scope is smaller than previously stated.
Examples include:
* protection survives only within a more restricted perturbative order;
* an EFT bridge is valid only in a reduced scale range;
* exact non-identification holds only on a subset of the original channels;
* a comparator result depends on a narrower nuisance or microphysical domain;
* a completion result applies only to one parameter region;
* a global falsifier holds only under a narrower cosmological branch.
Narrowing is not the same as refutation.
The result remains lawful inside the reduced scope.
The public correction should identify:
OLD SCOPE
NEW SCOPE
REASON FOR NARROWING
STATUS INSIDE NEW SCOPE
STATUS OUTSIDE NEW SCOPE
Outside the surviving scope, the result may become OPEN rather than FAIL.
Do not invent a stronger negative endpoint unless the evidence supports it.
8.4 Change Type 3 — EXTEND / NEW CASE
Use EXTEND / NEW CASE when the new work changes the scientific referent enough that it is no longer the same execution.
Common triggers include:
* a new mechanism;
* a materially changed mechanism;
* a new completion;
* a new comparator family;
* a new physical successor;
* a new observable family;
* a new channel set;
* a new physical domain;
* a new update class;
* a new numerical-realization claim;
* a broader claimed identification scope;
* a new theory-data inheritance rule.
The new case should receive:
* a new case identity;
* a new scientific registration;
* a new requiredness manifest;
* a new evidence record;
* and a new machine population.
The earlier result remains attached to the earlier referent.
New channel after exact non-identification
Suppose a mechanism was exactly non-identifiable on channels C₁.

That does not make the old result false.

Ordinarily:

8.5 Same-scope comparator defect versus genuinely new comparator
A new comparator deserves special care.
Most newly introduced comparators define an extension or new case.
But there is one important exception.
A newly noticed comparator may expose a defect in the original same-scope adjudication if:

* it addressed the same registered claim;
* it belonged to the scope the original analysis claimed to cover;
* and it should therefore have been included in the original comparator family.
In that case the challenge may be an OVERTURN or NARROW, not merely a new extension.
The central question is:
Was this comparator outside the original case, or was it an omitted member of the original case’s own declared comparison class?
The answer must be justified from the original freeze.
Do not decide after seeing which classification is rhetorically more convenient.

8.11 Within-case challenge surfaces
A critic may challenge the case without changing its scientific referent.
Requiredness and triggers
Challenge a seat marked NOT REQUIRED if the frozen claim actually depended on it, or challenge a required seat that has no job in the registered claim. A CONDITIONAL trigger must have been frozen before target-result access, be resolved by an independently adjudicable upstream condition, and remain independent of the desired terminal label. A hidden required burden can invalidate an execution; a spurious required burden can overconstrain it.
Procedure versus outcome
For mappability and degeneracyTest, distinguish the audit procedure from what the valid audit found. An invalid or non-diagnostic procedure can become VIOLATED or OPEN. A valid procedure with a wrongly reported outcome requires correction of the adjudication and any downstream SemanticPacket state; it does not make the completed procedure itself a violation.
Evidence, provenance, and case binding
Challenge whether each cited source supports the assigned proposition at the stated scope, whether evidence modes are described correctly, whether one structural identity is being double-counted as independent evidence, whether a terminal label is being recycled as its own prerequisite, and whether all released records genuinely bind to the same execution. A material defect reopens the affected burden or public machine claim even if the Lean file still compiles.
Aggregate status, governing blocker, and terminal coherence
Inspect the component burden vector rather than attacking or defending only the aggregate disposition. The governing blocker is the earliest unresolved or violated burden in the registered scientific dependency route that already prevents the requested inference; later blockers remain secondary. Human audit outcomes, top-level routing, and SemanticPacket terminal labels must describe one coherent case. Repair any contradiction at the lowest sufficient layer.
8.12 Result-specific challenge shortcuts

Exact non-identification

BELOW SENSITIVITY
Show that the original reach adjudication was wrong, that the residual is actually zero under the admitted comparator freedom, or that an upstream required burden was never closed. Improved reach ordinarily defines a new execution while leaving the earlier reach-limited result historically intact.
Completion-specific FAIL
Show that the same registered completion was misidentified, solved incorrectly, constrained incorrectly, or actually satisfies its original role under the original lawful freedom. A materially different completion is a new case.

Close the history burden itself: provide valid access to the required complete-history object, a demonstrably equivalent mechanism-owned relation, or a correction showing that the original claim did not require that history object as stated. Better precision on ordinary local observables does not close missing whole-history access.
Not refutations by themselves
Preference for a different mechanism, discovery of a new completion or channel, an OPEN burden, failure to derive a numerical value where no such claim was live, an untriggered scoped falsifier, proof-engineering cleanup, or the fact that machine-clean is not empirical confirmation does not refute the package. A refutation must reach an actual registered claim, dependency, or formal implication.
8.13 What would count as a strong framework-level challenge
The framework itself comes under stronger pressure if a specialist can exhibit a scientifically legitimate cosmological-constant case in which:
* the mechanism’s actual claim cannot be represented without distortion;
* the claim requires a burden that the framework cannot express;
* two scientifically distinct result states are forced into one state and that conflation changes the scientific conclusion;
* the framework necessarily requires irrelevant burdens and thereby makes a lawful claim impossible;
* the framework necessarily omits a burden required to distinguish a valid result from an invalid one;
* its claim-relative routing produces a false scientific implication;
* or its separation among protection, identification, completion viability, numerical realization, and physical access fails on a concrete case.
That would be substantive pressure.
The appropriate response would be to locate the defect and repair the lowest sufficient authority.
The framework is not protected from that possibility by construction.
8.14 Adversarial quick-reference matrix
Target: R₁ / R₂ protection PASS
To overturn:
Show the registered protected relation fails the original update challenge under the lawful frozen theory-data rules.
Does not overturn:
Local empirical non-identification.
Target: Exact non-identification
To overturn:
Show exact comparator equivalence is false under the same frozen scope.
To extend:
Introduce a genuinely new channel or broader comparator question.
Does not overturn:
Better precision on the same exactly degenerate channel.
Target: BELOW SENSITIVITY
To overturn:
Show the reach adjudication was wrong, the residual was actually zero, or an upstream burden was not closed.
To extend:
Run a higher-reach execution.
Does not imply:
Exact degeneracy.
Target: Whole-history OPEN
To close:
Supply the required history object or a scientifically demonstrated mechanism-owned equivalent.
Does not close:
Ordinary improvement in local measurement precision.
Target: Completion-specific FAIL
To overturn:
Show the same completion satisfies its original registered role or that the negative adjudication was invalid.
To extend:
Test a materially different completion.
Does not erase:
Independent parent protection merely by failing.
Target: Scoped one-way falsifier
To falsify branch:
Observe the predeclared incompatible state under the required assumptions.
Compatible outcome means:
The branch survives this test.
Compatible outcome does not mean:
Mechanism identified.
Target: Numerical realization NOT LIVE
To activate:
Supply an explicit physical value-selection / restriction / statistical-accounting claim.
Does not activate:
The mere existence of a measured numerical value.
Target: Adapter
To repair:
Demonstrate a real CCP-specific representation or routing defect.
Does not justify repair:
A difficult or negative scientific result.
Target: Universal Kernel
To reopen:
Demonstrate an unabsorbed, reusable, domain-neutral structural capacity required beyond CCP.
Does not reopen:
A cosmology-specific scientific burden.
8.15 The package should make disagreement productive
A useful adversarial response should end with one of a small number of concrete outcomes:
The earlier result stands.
The earlier result is overturned.
The earlier result survives but is narrowed.
The earlier result stands and the new work is a broader case.
An OPEN burden is now closed.
A scoped branch is falsified.
A previously non-live claim is now activated.
The public record is incoherent and requires repair.
The CCP adapter is insufficient and requires repair.
The Universal Kernel is under demonstrated reusable pressure.
That is preferable to generic disagreement.
The package is designed so that a critic can say not merely:
“this is wrong,”
but:
which object is wrong, what evidence defeats it, what stays fixed, and what status should change.
8.16 Compression
The adversarial rule is:
Challenge the smallest object that the evidence actually reaches.
New evidence should overturn an old result only when it defeats the old result under its own referent.
A broader question normally creates a broader case.
A failed completion does not erase parent protection.
A newly accessible history can close OPEN without guaranteeing PASS.
A new value-selection mechanism activates a new claim rather than retroactively creating one.
An adapter defect belongs to the adapter.
A reusable domain-neutral structural defect may place pressure on the kernel.
Repair at the lowest sufficient authority.
Nothing in the package is made immune to challenge by being machine-clean.
9. Public Execution Release Manifest
A completed internal adjudication is not yet a reproducible public result.
A public execution must release enough information for an independent group to reconstruct the scientific case, see how the evidence populated the contract, reproduce the machine population, identify any departures from the frozen protocol, and understand exactly what the resulting claim does and does not establish.
This section therefore defines the minimum public release manifest for an execution of the CCP contract.
It is not another scientific registration form. It does not repeat the 22-burden dictionary. It does not replace the operating procedure or the machine-interpretation rules. Those objects already exist earlier in this companion.
Its job is narrower:
bind the scientific registration, evidence record, machine population, analysis artifacts, and released claim to one auditable execution.
A release should contain the seven release blocks below, followed by the final human coherence attestation.
9.1 Identity
Every public execution begins by identifying exactly what was run and what scientific case it belongs to.
Record:
PACKAGE / RELEASE IDENTITY
The public package or release to which this execution belongs.
CASE IDENTIFIER
A stable human-readable identifier for the scientific case.
EXECUTION IDENTIFIER
The identifier for this particular run, adjudication, or released execution.
A single scientific case may have more than one execution. Do not rely on the case identifier alone when later data, calculations, protocol revisions, or adjudications create a distinct execution.
MECHANISM / MODEL IDENTIFIER
The exact mechanism, theory, construction, branch, or completion under review.
CASE PHASE
One of:
* planned;
* in execution;
* completed / adjudicated.
Case phase is a human scientific record. It must not be inferred from ExperimentReadyForEmpiricalRun.
That predicate means only that the machine-required seats in the supplied versioned ledger snapshot satisfy the encoded closure condition. It is not by itself a chronological acquisition decision, an empirical result, or a statement of final scientific truth.
SCIENTIFIC ANCHOR IDENTITY
Exact title, version, release identifier, or other stable reference for the scientific anchor governing the execution.
COMPANION IDENTITY
Exact version or release identity of this companion.
CCP ADAPTER IDENTITY
Exact adapter filename / release identity used for the machine execution.
UNIVERSAL KERNEL IDENTITY
Exact compatible Universal Kernel filename / release identity used by the adapter.
SCIENTIFIC REGISTRATION / FREEZE IDENTITY
The exact preregistration, scientific case record, or protocol freeze governing the execution.
DATE / RELEASE IDENTIFIER
Include where needed to distinguish successive executions or public releases.
Use cryptographic hashes where practical for files whose exact contents matter to reproduction.
Stable explanatory prose elsewhere in the companion need not hard-code transient development filenames. Exact execution binding belongs here.
9.2 Frozen Scientific Registration
Release the complete frozen scientific registration governing the case, or give an exact stable reference to the released record.
Do not replace that record with a short retrospective summary.
The registration should bind, where applicable:
* mechanism and scientific claim identity;
* declared physical scope and applicability domain;
* ClaimProfile;
* empirical adjudication type;
* UpdateClass;
* protected functional Πₘ;
* reference theory data θ₀ or allowed region Θ₀;
* free-datum ledger;
* source object;
* actual physical successor variation ΔXₛ;
* pre-state, successor state, and physical transition where Sₘ is live;
* lawful parameter-flow or inheritance maps;
* scale / state bridge;
* SuccessorExperimentRoute;
* frozen channel set;
* forward-model declaration;
* comparator family and permitted freedom;
* serious alternatives where required by the claim;
* mappability / exact-degeneracy audit rule;
* sensitivity or reach rule where sensitivity is live;
* data and reference-data rules;
* uncertainty and material theory-dependence treatment;
* and the frozen result rule.
The public manifest need not restate all of those fields in full if the complete frozen registration is itself released.
Instead record:
FROZEN REGISTRATION
Exact file / record identifier, version, release location, and hash where practical.
The scientific registration remains upstream of the machine population.
A later machine state cannot retroactively create the scientific object, comparator, channel, criterion, or evidence rule that was supposed to have been frozen before target-result access.
9.3 Human Burden / Evidence Manifest
Where Sₘ is live, release the complete human requiredness and evidence record in addition to the encoded Lean ledger.
For each of the 22 possible Sₘ burdens, record enough information to reconstruct why the burden was or was not required and how its status was adjudicated.
Use the requiredness grammar:
ALWAYS
The burden is constitutive of every live CCP physical-successor case.
CONDITIONAL(trigger)
The burden becomes required if the stated frozen upstream condition is satisfied.
NOT REQUIRED
The frozen claim and route do not require the burden.
The first eight Sₘ burdens are constitutive of every live physical-successor case and therefore cannot be encoded as absent merely because their scientific answer is simple:
1. realChange
2. protectedSectorMembership
3. piRelevantChange
4. mechanismApplicability
5. inheritedTheoryData
6. variationConnection
7. falsificationConnection
8. freeDatumStability
If one of these is scientifically trivial in a particular case, discharge it with an explicit scientific basis. Do not represent triviality as non-requirement.
For each burden, record as applicable:
BURDEN
Exact machine burden name.
REQUIREDNESS
ALWAYS / CONDITIONAL(trigger) / NOT REQUIRED.
STATUS
Where required:
* DISCHARGED;
* VIOLATED;
* OPEN.
A genuinely non-required burden may map to none.
A required unresolved burden is OPEN, not none.
SCIENTIFIC SOURCE
The paper, equation, calculation, dataset, simulation, preregistration, measurement, or other admissible source bearing on the burden.
SUPPORTED PROPOSITION
The exact scientific proposition that the cited evidence supports.
Do not let a terminal result label substitute for the independent support that licenses that same result.
EVIDENTIARY ROLE
For example: source definition, applicability support, forward-model validation, comparator support, procedure completion, reach determination, data support, or result-freeze evidence.
SCOPE
The mechanism version, parameter region, epoch, physical regime, channel set, dataset, approximation, or other limit that travels with the adjudication.
EVIDENCE PHASE
Where material:
* forecast / planned;
* achieved / observed;
* post-execution audit.
Phase-sensitive burdens must be re-audited before a completed public result is released.
ADJUDICATION OUTCOME
Where the burden is an audit or procedure seat, record the scientific result returned by the completed procedure separately from the procedure disposition.
This is especially important for mappability and degeneracyTest.
For those seats:
* DISCHARGED means the frozen audit was validly completed;
* VIOLATED means the registered procedure was invalid or non-diagnostic;
* OPEN means the procedure remains unresolved or incomplete.
DISCHARGED does not itself say whether exact mapping or exact degeneracy exists.
The scientific outcome belongs in the human adjudication record and, where appropriate, the terminal SemanticPacket.
Requiredness Freeze Record
Also publish the requiredness freeze governing the execution.
At minimum record:
* the exact conditional triggers;
* the preregistration / freeze identity;
* version and hash where practical;
* the final resolution of each conditional trigger;
* the resulting machine requiredness mapping;
* and the post-run comparison between the frozen rules and the population actually used.
This record makes visible not merely what the final Lean ledger contains, but why each some _ or none was lawful.

9.6 Freeze, Deviation, and Case-Binding Audit
A preregistration proves only that a rule was frozen at one point in time.
A public result must also show what happened afterward.
Release:
PRE-EXECUTION FREEZE RECORD
The exact identity and version of the scientific registration, protocol, requiredness rules, analysis rule, and result rule frozen before target adjudication.
PREDECLARED ADAPTIVE BRANCHES
Any conditional analysis branch, trigger, allowed protocol adaptation, or decision rule that was part of the frozen design.
POST-EXECUTION DEVIATION AUDIT
State either:
NO MATERIAL DEVIATION
or identify every material deviation.
For each deviation record:
* what changed;
* when it changed;
* whether target-result access had already occurred;
* whether the change was predeclared;
* whether it was pre-result;
* whether it was non-substantive;
* whether it changed the scientific claim, comparator, channel, threshold, evidence rule, or decision rule;
* its effect on the released result;
* whether the execution remains admissible under the original freeze;
* and whether the change required a new execution identifier.
A material post-result change that alters the claim or decision rule must not be hidden inside the old execution.

* scientific registration;
* CasePacket;
* human requiredness manifest;

* SemanticPacket;
* SuccessorExperimentCase;
* evidence and analysis artifacts;
* and machine output.
Use exact versions, release identifiers, and hashes where practical.

That record-identity burden belongs in the public manifest.
Negative Results
For every negative result, explicitly report:
FAILED / BLOCKING OBJECT
The exact completion, burden, route, procedure, support condition, or other object that failed or blocked the claim.
PARENT / PARALLEL STATUS NOT CHANGED
Any independently established result that the negative finding does not erase.
Do not promote a local failure into a broader failure merely because the objects are related.
Where more than one OPEN or VIOLATED burden exists, additionally report:
GOVERNING BLOCKER
The burden that currently prevents the relevant adjudication most directly.
SECONDARY BLOCKERS
Other live unresolved or violated burdens that would still require repair if the governing blocker were removed.
9.7 Claim Ceiling
Every released execution ends with three explicit statements.
SCIENTIFIC RESULT
State the narrowest scientific sentence supported by the domain evidence.
This is the physics-facing result.
MACHINE RESULT
State what the clean adapter execution, support predicate, license predicate, or theorem establishes about the supplied machine population at its declared scope.
This is the formal conditional result.
It must not be written as though the machine independently supplied the scientific premises.
NON-CLAIM / CEILING
State the nearest tempting stronger conclusion that the release does not license.
A typical pattern is:
SCIENTIFIC RESULT
The frozen local-history channels are exactly reproduced by the admitted matched comparator under the registered comparison.
MACHINE RESULT
The populated case is licensed as exact non-identification at the encoded scope and cannot be promoted to empirical-discrimination PASS.
NON-CLAIM / CEILING
This does not establish universal observational equivalence and does not erase an independently supported protection result.
The exact language will vary by case. The three-part separation should not.
Status Changes
If the release changes a previously public scientific status, append the status-change record defined in Section 8:
TARGET
What prior object or claim is under challenge.
CHANGE TYPE
Refute / reopen / extend / supersede / repair, as applicable.
HELD FIXED
The scope, referent, mechanism version, comparator, channel, or other conditions retained from the earlier result.
CHANGED
The evidence, channel, calculation, model, assumption, procedure, or machine object that changed.
EVIDENCE
The source support for the change.
RESULTING ACTION
The exact status change licensed by that evidence.
A broader new question normally creates a broader case. It does not silently rewrite a narrower prior result.
9.8 Final Human Coherence Attestation
Before release, a responsible human adjudicator should attest that the released scientific record and machine population still describe one coherent execution.
At minimum confirm:
* the human audit outcomes agree with the terminal SemanticPacket labels;
* the top-level routing state agrees with the Sₘ case population;
* requiredness followed the frozen ALWAYS / CONDITIONAL(trigger) / NOT REQUIRED rules;
* conditional triggers were resolved according to the frozen record;
* evidence provenance matches the evidence actually used;
* procedure-seat outcomes are not being confused with procedure dispositions;
* phase-sensitive evidence was re-audited where required;
* the scientific registration, evidence record, machine population, and machine output bind to the same execution;
* and the public claim does not exceed either the scientific evidence or the machine license.
This attestation is not a substitute for independent reproduction.
It closes a different burden: that the released package is internally bound strongly enough for another group to know what, exactly, it is being asked to reproduce.
The public execution manifest therefore carries one final rule:
release the scientific case that was actually adjudicated, the evidence that actually supported it, the machine population that actually represented it, and the narrowest claim that actually follows.
Anything less weakens the audit trail precisely where the contract is supposed to make it strongest.

A.4 Status Mapping and Non-Equivalences
The adapter contains more than one status grammar.
They must not be collapsed.
Top-Level Routing Grammar
BurdenStatus
* PASS
* FAIL
* NOT LIVE
* UNRESOLVED
Sₘ Experiment-Seat Grammar
ExperimentDisposition
* DISCHARGED
* VIOLATED
* OPEN
* none when genuinely non-required
These grammars do different jobs.
The following non-equivalences are mandatory.
none ≠ OPEN
none means the burden is genuinely non-required.
OPEN means the burden is required and unresolved.
DISCHARGED ≠ experimental PASS
DISCHARGED means the burden carried by that seat has been adequately closed under its scientific rule.
It does not mean the experiment as a whole passed.
This distinction is especially important for procedure seats.
A valid mappability audit that establishes exact mapping is DISCHARGED.
A valid mappability audit that excludes exact mapping is also DISCHARGED.
The disposition records completion of the audit.
The human adjudication outcome records what the audit found.
VIOLATED ≠ automatic whole-case FAIL
VIOLATED means that the burden represented by that seat failed under its registered criterion.
Whether that violation defeats:
* one support route;
* one completion;
* one branch;
* one empirical claim;
* or a broader parent result
depends on the frozen scientific dependency structure.
Do not promote the violation beyond the object it actually reaches.
notIdentifiable ≠ evidence for exact mapping
ApplicabilityState.notIdentifiable records the supplied terminal adjudication.
It cannot be recycled as evidence that the mappability or degeneracy burden was independently discharged.
The lawful direction is:
independent comparator / mapping audit
→ scientific exact-non-identification adjudication
→ notIdentifiable machine state.
belowSensitivity ≠ proof of residual size
A ChannelRelation of below sensitivity records the supplied domain adjudication that a nonzero residual survives but lies below the frozen reach.
The machine label does not itself measure the residual.
The scientific analysis must supply that result.
Sensitivity Non-Requirement ≠ Exact Non-Identification
For exact non-identification, sensitivity becomes non-required only downstream.
The lawful direction is:
exact mapping / degeneracy adjudication
→ NOT IDENTIFIABLE
→ sensitivity non-required.
Never infer:
sensitivity = none
→ therefore NOT IDENTIFIABLE.
A.5 Manifest-First Population Workflow
Section 5 is the governing full execution procedure. For machine-reference purposes, the order compresses to:
1. complete and freeze the human scientific registration;
2. freeze requiredness and every conditional trigger;
3. adjudicate each required evidence burden from independent scientific support;
4. transcribe the adjudicated record into the machine constructors;
5. run the human/machine coherence audit;
6. run only the support, license, or routing check corresponding to the scientific endpoint being released;
7. publish the relevant machine output separately from the scientific result.
Lean construction is transcription of an adjudicated record, not scientific discovery by constructor selection.

A.8 Machine Ceiling
The adapter checks encoded conditional consequences of supplied scientific records: routing consistency, requiredness consequences, support and case-license conditions, non-implication firewalls, fixture properties, and lawful coexistence of machine states.
It does not independently establish physical truth, provenance authenticity, correctness of calculations, adequacy of comparators or datasets, honesty of freeze records, physical identity of records, citation fidelity, or that the populated case accurately describes nature.
The public interpretation is therefore:
physics and domain analysis supply the premises and scientific adjudication; the CCP adapter checks the encoded support, license, and routing consequences of that supplied record.
A clean machine result makes conditional reasoning reproducible and auditable. It does not turn conditional consistency into empirical truth.
